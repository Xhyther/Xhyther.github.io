## Why I Write

I believe one of the best ways to learn something is to **explain it to someone else**.

That is one of the main reasons I started this blog.

Here, I document things I build, problems I encounter, CTF challenges I solve, and concepts I’m trying to understand. Some posts may be polished project writeups; others may simply be notes from something I spent several hours figuring out.

The goal isn't to pretend I already know everything.

It's to **build a record of what I learn**.

Hopefully, some of it will be useful to someone else along the way.
